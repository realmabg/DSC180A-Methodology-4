**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic has been how a basketball player’s performance changes after transferring schools. A player’s statistics depend on their role, teammates, and competition, which makes it difficult to predict whether their success will carry over. I am interested in how player archetypes can help explain differences that overall performance metrics might miss.

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate which types of transfers are most likely to succeed at mid-major programs. This could involve examining how a player’s style fits the destination team’s roster and needs, alongside their previous performance. Since I work with the UCSD basketball team, I am interested in making the results useful for recruiting decisions at programs with similar resources.

**What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

I would put more emphasis on changes in playing time and role when evaluating transfer outcomes. A decline in scoring does not necessarily mean a player became less effective; they may have fewer opportunities on their new team. Separating changes in opportunity from changes in efficiency could help us better understand which transfers performed well.

**What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in using nearest-neighbor methods to identify past transfers with similar profiles and see how they performed at their destinations. I would also like to explore hierarchical models that account for differences across teams and conferences. These could help us make predictions while showing the uncertainty involved, especially for players with fewer comparable examples.
